What changed from v1:

  • The dock is transient. It costs ~15% of a 1080p viewport and was previously spent even when nothing was selected. Now it's summoned by selection and dismissed with × or Escape — which is what your objection was actually pointing at.
  • Cinematic mode exists. You were right that floating/full-bleed serves the map better in some states. That state is directorManager.js driving the camera, and it now gets the whole frame with a single low-contrast corner readout.
  • Rails stay. 11.0% of viewport at 1920×1080, in fixed regions, never covering a pick target — a much better trade than 15 unpredictable floating panels, and it lets the camera know its own visible rect. Inspect mode costs 25.1%, which is exactly why it's transient.
  • Regions are named by meaning — global-state, tools, selection, time — not by screen edge. That's what lets the same architecture render as a bezel here and as world-locked slabs in AR. See ui-ar.html.
