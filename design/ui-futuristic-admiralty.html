What changed, and why it's the effectiveness fix rather than a paint job:

  • The AIS gap is a hole in a trace, not a badge. You see the shape of the problem — reports, then nothing, then reports — before reading a single character. The old version spelled AIS GAP 4h 12m and made you parse it.
  • Bearing is a dial. 213° means nothing at a glance; a needle pointing south-west means something instantly. Same pixels, ~8× faster read.
  • Feed rows carry four channels in one strip — name, health pip, throughput histogram, count. The degraded cable feed is distinguishable at a squint with no text at all.
  • Layer rows show the glyph they actually draw. The legend and the control collapse into one object.
  • Frame time is a sparkline with the hitch marked amber. A number can't show you a hitch.
  • The only animation encodes state — the live sweep runs on healthy feeds and stops on degraded ones. Contrast with the nine decorative @keyframes pulses in index.html, which fire regardless of what's true.
  • "Futuristic" is carried by registration marks, graduated scale rules, sounding numerals and tabular figures — precision cues. No blur, no bloom, no glow. Nothing here would read as a movie prop.
